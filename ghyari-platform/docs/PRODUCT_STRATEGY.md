## Executive Summary / الملخص التنفيذي

**غياري** (Ghyari — meaning "my parts" / "my gear" in colloquial Arabic) is positioned to become the dominant Arabic-language automotive parts marketplace in the GCC region. We are not building another generic e-commerce site — we are building the cultural home of Arab car culture, powered by AI and wrapped in a premium 3D visual experience.

**The core insight:** Arab car enthusiasts — especially the youth segment — are deeply passionate, highly social, and chronically underserved by existing solutions. The current market is fragmented across WhatsApp groups, local shops, and foreign platforms with no Arabic support.

**Our answer:** A platform that speaks their language (literally), understands their cars (compatibility engine), predicts what they need (AI radar), and gives them a visual experience worthy of their passion (3D UI).

## The 5 Core Pillars / المحاور الخمسة الأساسية

### Pillar 1: Consumables (قطع الاستهلاك السريع)
**What it is:** The bread-and-butter of automotive retail — tires, brakes, oil, filters, batteries.

**Why it matters:** This is the high-frequency purchase category that builds customer habit and lifetime value. A customer who buys oil filters monthly becomes a lifetime customer who also buys performance upgrades.

**Strategy:**
- **Price leadership** on top-10 high-velocity SKUs (Castrol oil, Bosch batteries, Michelin tires)
- **Bundle offers:** "Complete maintenance kit for your Toyota Camry 2022"
- **Subscription model (Phase 2):** "Your oil change kit, delivered every 5,000km"
- **Urgency signals:** Stock levels, "3 left in your city" messaging

**Key Brands to Stock:**
- Tires: Bridgestone, Michelin, Yokohama, Toyo, Continental
- Brakes: Brembo, Akebono, Hawk Performance, EBC
- Oils: Motul, Castrol, Mobil 1, Total, Shell
- Filters: K&N, Mann+Hummel, MAHLE, Hengst
- Batteries: Varta, Bosch, Optima, Century, MF

**Success Metrics:**
- 40% of total GMV from consumables in Year 1
- Average order frequency: 4x/year per customer
- Repeat purchase rate: > 60%

### Pillar 2: Performance & Tuning (قطع الأداء — نيسان فتك)
**What it is:** High-margin performance upgrades, tuning parts, JDM imports, and modification kits.

**Why it matters:** This is the emotional, identity-driven purchase category. Performance enthusiasts are brand evangelists who share their builds on social media, driving organic growth. Margins are 2-3x higher than consumables.

**The Nissan Focus (نيسان فتك — Nissan Fatak):**
"فتك" (fatak) is Arabic street slang meaning something is wildly fast/aggressive. The Nissan tuning scene in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf is massive — the Patrol (Y60/Y61/Y62), Skyline, 350Z/370Z, and GT-R are cultural icons.

**Nissan Performance Catalog:**
- **Patrol Y62 (VK56):** Cold air intakes, intercoolers, ECU tunes, lift kits, skid plates, ARB accessories
- **GT-R R35:** HKS turbos, Trust intercoolers, Nismo parts, AMS performance kits
- **350Z/370Z (Z33/Z34):** Stillen kits, Tomei exhausts, Tein suspension, Work Wheels
- **Skyline (R32/R33/R34):** Genuine Nismo parts, HKS builds, Cusco chassis components
- **Frontier/Navara:** Desert running boards, snorkels, roof racks, UCAs

**Other Performance Verticals:**
- Toyota Land Cruiser (80/100/200/300 series): ARB, Kings Springs, Ironman 4x4
- Toyota Supra (A90): GReddy, Titan Motorsports, Chargespeed
- Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution: Perrin, Ralliart, STM
- Subaru Impreza WRX/STI: Perrin, Cobb Tuning, Kartboy

**Strategy:**
- Build the **definitive Arabic-language resource** for JDM parts in the Gulf
- Partner with **local tuning shops** as verified installers
- Create **car build showcase** social feature — "شوف بنيتي" (See My Build)
- Video content: "كيف تزود باترول؟" style tutorial content

**Success Metrics:**
- 35% of total GMV from performance parts in Year 2
- Average order value (AOV): SAR 850+ (performance) vs SAR 180 (consumables)
- Community builds posted: 500+ in Year 1

### Pillar 3: Local Distributor Network (شبكة الموزعين)
**What it is:** A vetted network of local automotive parts distributors integrated into the platform.

**Why it matters:** Ghyari is not a warehouse — we are a marketplace. Local distributors hold physical inventory and can deliver within 24-48 hours. We provide them the digital storefront they lack.

**Distributor Value Proposition:**
- Digital presence (most operate only on WhatsApp)
- Access to 100,000+ online customers
- AI demand signals showing what to stock
- Standardized order management system

**Distributor Onboarding Requirements:**
1. Valid commercial registration (سجل تجاري)
2. Physical warehouse/showroom location
3. Minimum 500 active SKUs
4. Commitment to 24-48h order fulfillment
5. Accept return policy standards

**Geographic Coverage (Year 1 targets):**
- Riyadh: 8 distributors
- Jeddah: 5 distributors
- Dammam/Al Khobar: 4 distributors
- Dubai: 3 distributors
- Abu Dhabi: 2 distributors

**Revenue Share Model:**
- Standard distributor: 12% commission
- Premium distributor (verified, 5-star): 10% commission (incentive for quality)
- Featured placement: SAR 500/month flat fee

**Success Metrics:**
- 30 active distributors in Year 1
- Distributor NPS > 60
- Average distributor GMV: SAR 50,000/month

### Pillar 4: AI Radar System (رادار الذكاء الاصطناعي)
**What it is:** An intelligent demand detection system that captures what customers want but can't find, analyzes patterns, and automatically suggests new inventory.

**Why it matters:** In a fragmented market, the platform that knows what customers want before they leave is the platform that wins. The AI Radar turns customer frustration (not finding a product) into actionable business intelligence.

**How It Works:**

```
Step 1 — Signal Capture
  → Customer searches for "كتمان صوت GTR" (GT-R exhaust tip)
  → Not found in catalog
  → System records: query, car model, user profile, timestamp

Step 2 — Signal Aggregation
  → 47 searches for GT-R exhaust parts in 30 days
  → Cross-referenced with: user locations, price willingness, car year
  
Step 3 — Claude AI Analysis
  → Prompt to Claude: "47 users searched for GT-R R35 exhaust components
    in Saudi Arabia over 30 days. Current inventory: zero. Analyze and
    recommend: product specs, supplier sources, pricing, priority score."
  → Claude returns structured JSON with recommendation
  
Step 4 — Auto-Pull Job
  → System generates procurement task
  → Assigned to sourcing team with supplier contacts
  → Timeline: 2-week target to list product
  
Step 5 — Customer Notification
  → Users who searched get WhatsApp/email: "القطعة اللي طلبتها وصلت!"
    ("The part you requested has arrived!")
  → Creates conversion event and loyalty signal
```

**Arabic NLP Capabilities:**
- Gulf dialect understanding (Saudi, Emirati, Kuwaiti)
- Egyptian and Levantine dialect support (expatriate workers)
- Car model name variations (فوكس = Focus, لاندكروزر = Land Cruiser)
- Part name aliases (بريك = brake, دوبل = driveshaft)
- Transliteration handling (GTR, جي تي ار, جتر)

**Success Metrics:**
- Demand capture rate: >85% of unfulfilled searches recorded
- Signal-to-listing conversion: 40% within 30 days
- Notification open rate: >45% (WhatsApp)
- Re-engagement conversion: >25%

### Pillar 5: 3D Immersive UI (واجهات ثلاثية الأبعاد)
**What it is:** A visually stunning, Three.js-powered interface that makes browsing parts feel like being in a premium automotive showroom.

**Why it matters:** In a commodity market, experience is the differentiator. The target customer (18-35, male, car enthusiast) is highly visual, consumes automotive content on YouTube and Instagram, and responds to premium aesthetic experiences.

**3D Experience Architecture:**

| Page | 3D Experience | Technical Implementation |
|------|---------------|--------------------------|
| Home/Hero | Rotating brake caliper with particle trails | Three.js + custom shader |
| Category Landing | Animated engine bay scene | GLTF models + react-three/fiber |
| Product Detail | 360° part viewer with annotations | OrbitControls + Raycaster |
| Nissan Zone | Full car silhouette with hot-spot parts | Custom interactive scene |
| Search Results | 3D grid with tilt hover effect | CSS3D transforms |
| Cart | Floating parts assembly animation | Framer Motion 3D |

**Performance Strategy:**
- Mobile-first: Detect GPU capability, serve 2D fallback on low-end devices
- DRACO compression: All GLB models compressed at >80%
- Streaming: Progressive loading, show placeholder during 3D load
- Lazy loading: 3D only initializes when element is in viewport

## User Personas / شخصيات المستخدمين

### Persona 1: محمد — عشاق السيارات (The Enthusiast)
**Profile:**
- Age: 22, single, living in Riyadh
- Car: Nissan Patrol Y62, modified
- Income: SAR 8,000/month (entry-level job + father support)
- Digital behavior: Heavy Instagram/TikTok, YouTube car content, WhatsApp groups

**Motivations:**
- Wants his Patrol to stand out — performance mods, lifted, aggressive look
- Deep knowledge of JDM culture; watches Japanese car shows
- Social status through car — "الباترول هو الشخصية"

- Can't find specific JDM parts locally → orders from Japan at huge shipping cost

- Can't verify compatibility without calling the shop

**Ghyari Solution:**
- Nissan performance vertical with verified JDM parts
- Compatibility checker (enters Y62, sees all compatible mods)
- Verified distributor badges + reviews
- Build showcase to share mods

**Buying Trigger:** "أبي قطع موثوقة لباترولي بدون ما أدور في الواتساب"
("I want verified parts for my Patrol without hunting through WhatsApp")

## Go-to-Market Strategy / استراتيجية الذهاب للسوق

### Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-3) — المرحلة الأولى
**Focus:** Riyadh, Saudi Arabia only

**Launch Strategy:**
1. **Beta with 50 enthusiasts:** Recruit from Nissan Patrol WhatsApp groups / Instagram
2. **Content seeding:** Launch YouTube channel with Nissan build guides (Arabic)
3. **5 anchor distributors in Riyadh** integrated pre-launch
4. **1,000 SKU catalog:** Focus on top-moving Patrol, Land Cruiser, Camry parts

**Channel Strategy:**
- Instagram (@ghyari.sa): Daily content — builds, mods, Arabic car culture
- TikTok (@ghyari): Short-form "شوف جديد غياري" (See what's new at Ghyari) videos
- YouTube: Long-form build guides, "كيف تزود سيارتك" (How to upgrade your car)
- WhatsApp Business: Broadcast list for deals and new arrivals
- SEO: Target Arabic automotive keywords (zero competition currently)

### Phase 2: Growth (Months 4-6) — المرحلة الثانية
**Focus:** Expand to Jeddah + UAE (Dubai)

**Tactics:**
- Launch **"بناء الشهر" (Build of the Month)** competition — Instagram virality
- **Workshop partner program:** 20 Riyadh workshops as verified installers
- **Google Shopping:** Arabic product listings
- **Influencer partnerships:** Top Saudi car YouTubers (@something, etc.)
- **Ramadan campaign:** "زود سيارتك في رمضان" — strong gifting/treating behavior

### Phase 3: Scale (Months 7-12) — المرحلة الثالثة
**Focus:** Full GCC, introduce Trade accounts and AI Radar Pro

**Tactics:**
- **B2B sales team:** Direct outreach to large workshops and fleet managers
- **OEM partnerships:** Approach Nissan Arabia, Toyota Arabia for certified parts
- **Mobile app launch** (React Native)
- **API partnerships:** Offer parts data API to insurance companies, fleet managers
